# Baseline matrix on olympus, two SPUs side by side, stack 9608e5b, 2026-10-01

## The claim

**Each SPU reproduced every seeded session it served, under its held tuple.** Two
agents served the same declaration, apart from their device and trace lines, on the two
RTX A6000s of one box for seven hours. rustbase ran the Rust SPU (the native candle engine,
BF16). pyra ran python-spu (transformers eager, bfloat16). Both runs exited 0, with no
divergence and no error. Sampling is stochastic. What the run shows is that a seeded
stochastic run reproduces exactly when every field of the deployment tuple is held. It
does not show that the system is deterministic.

## The cross-SPU reading

The two runs differ in one tuple field, the kernel stack: candle's BF16 forward against
torch's, with python-spu's sampler a draw-for-draw port of the Rust one. The tuple predicts
that bits move with it, and they do:

- **Turns:** 72 of the 464 turn slots carry the same emission on both SPUs.
  - All 32 opening filler turns match.
  - 8 of the 32 probe turns match.
- **Cells:** only 3 of the 32 are identical end to end on both SPUs: factual-short,
  arithmetic and definition, each at depth 2.
- **Where they part:** in every differing cell the outputs part at the second turn and stay
  apart.
- **Within each SPU, every cell is stable:** the parting is between kernel stacks, never
  within one.

This is a reading of two results, not a third probe. The cards are two dies of one model,
not one card used in turn, so it does not isolate the kernel stack from the die. Two dies
of this model have reproduced each other before (2026-09-28, karl and fred).

## How the run was reached

Three smokes ran before the night, all side by side:

1. **Refused every session.** The device reader took an empty unit journal for one this
   user could not read.
2. **Refused again.** Measured as root, neither engine writes to the journal under its
   invocation; only systemd's own entries are there. #8 then made readability a positive
   control: systemd's start entry for the invocation. A quiet engine is now recorded as
   printing no device line, and the binding stands on the unit's processes crossed with
   `nvidia-smi`.
3. **Passed** at 07eb045: 11 of 11 and 5 of 5 reproduced.

Both refusals failed closed: no guessed device was recorded.

## What this run does not establish

- **No loop, no state member.** As in Spec section 8, the agents declare neither, so a
  result says nothing about replay through a loop that edits context, nor about recall
  or restored records.
- **Most turns generate one token**, so the substantive generations are counted apart,
  above.
- **One model, one precision.** Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct at BF16. This is the instrument
  holding on an easy target, on both SPUs.
- **Batch composition is one by construction.**
- **python-spu's speed is not a result.** Its sessions run 2 to 20 times longer than the
  Rust SPU's. Its sampler is a pure-Python, bit-exact port, and each load imports torch.
  That is carried as WeaverAgents#25's speed item. A faster sampler is a new kernel-stack
  value and owes its own run.
- **python-spu's weights walk predates WeaverAgents#44.** On a plain directory the two
  walks agree, so the same `w` serves both. On a directory with links or dot-entries they
  would not.
